# PURPOSE

This document defines the current development trajectory of SIR
(Structural Integrity Recursion).

The purpose of SIR is not direct implementation of AI alignment systems.

The purpose is the mathematical and structural investigation of:

- recursive integrity,
- admissibility-preserving evolution,
- recursive consistency,
- bounded recursive systems,
- and structural stability under recursive transformation.

SIR operates as a foundational recursive integrity framework.
