# Limitations

## Purpose

The present SIR framework is exploratory and incomplete.

The current formulation defines only minimal recursive integrity structures and does not yet constitute a finalized mathematical theory.

---

# No Universal Integrity Metric

The framework does not currently define a unique or universal integrity metric.

The quantity:

\[
I(t)
\]

remains abstract and domain-dependent.

Different systems may require different operational definitions of integrity.

---

# No Complete Dynamical Formalism

The update operator:

\[
U(t)
\]

is currently defined only at an abstract structural level.

No universal dynamical equations have yet been derived.

---

# No Proven Convergence Theorems

The framework does not currently provide:

- convergence proofs,
- global stability theorems,
- or universal boundedness guarantees.

Recursive stability remains an open research problem.

---

# Limited Observability

The framework explicitly assumes incomplete observability.

Observed inconsistency:

\[
\hat{D}(t)
\]

may differ significantly from actual inconsistency:

\[
D(t)
\]

This limitation may restrict empirical reconstruction.

---

# No Consciousness Claim

The framework does not claim to explain consciousness, subjective experience, or self-awareness.

Possible applications to cognitive systems remain speculative and incomplete.

---

# No Domain Equivalence

Although the framework is structurally substrate-independent, no equivalence is assumed between:

- physical systems,
- biological systems,
- cognitive systems,
- computational systems,
- or social systems.

Structural similarity does not imply ontological equivalence.

---

# No Physical Unification Claim

The framework does not claim to unify:

- physics,
- cognition,
- intelligence,
- or social organization

within a single finalized theory.

Possible structural parallels remain hypothetical.

---

# No Guaranteed Alignment Solution

Applications of recursive integrity principles to AI alignment or governance systems remain conceptual.

The framework does not currently provide a proven alignment solution for advanced AI systems.

---

# Open Mathematical Problems

Important unresolved mathematical problems include:

- formal integrity metrics,
- recursive convergence conditions,
- observability bounds,
- scaling laws,
- and stability criteria for highly recursive systems.

---

# Scientific Status

SIR should currently be understood as:

- a formal exploratory framework,
- a recursive systems model,
- and a structural integrity formalism under active development.
