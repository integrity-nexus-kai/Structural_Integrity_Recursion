# Refinement Stability Scope and Limits

## Purpose

This document defines the explicit scope, limitations, and non-claims of the refinement-stability framework.

The objective is NOT expanding the framework.

Instead, the purpose is preventing uncontrolled conceptual inflation and maintaining strict separation between:

- structural hypotheses,
- exploratory models,
- and established mathematical results.

This document represents the first explicit boundary-definition layer of the refinement-stability program.

---

# 1. Motivation

The refinement-stability framework progressively evolved from:

- TIG structural analysis,
- low-energy operator fragmentation studies,
- refinement dynamics,
- and organizational stability hypotheses.

As frameworks grow structurally deeper, a major scientific risk emerges:

```text
conceptual expansion
outpacing mathematical control.
```

The purpose of the present document is therefore:

```text
scope stabilization.
```

The framework currently investigates only the following central structural question:
