# Refinement Stability Research Program

## Purpose

This document outlines a possible long-term research program for investigating the refinement-stability framework emerging from the structural comparison between TIG and the Yang–Mills Interface program.

The objective is NOT constructing a finalized universal theory.

Instead, the purpose is defining a systematic pathway for transforming the refinement-stability hypothesis into a mathematically investigable research direction.

This document represents the first explicit programmatic roadmap of the refinement-stability framework.

---

Previous development introduced:

- recursive refinement dynamics,
- fragmentation pressure,
- low-energy operator organization,
- refinement attractors,
- fragmentation observables,
- minimal refinement axioms,
- and explicit falsifiability criteria.

The framework now asks:

```text
How could these ideas
```

The refinement-stability framework currently adopts the following cautious position:

