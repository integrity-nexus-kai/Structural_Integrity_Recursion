# Publication Strategy — SIR

## Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR)

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the publication and positioning strategy of the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is the controlled development of a mathematically governed recursive admissibility framework while preserving scientific clarity, semantic stability, and publication compatibility.

---

# 2. Strategic Principle

SIR follows a layered publication strategy:

> first stabilization → then consolidation → then publication

The framework should evolve through controlled mathematical refinement rather than uncontrolled conceptual expansion.

---

# 3. Current Position

SIR is currently positioned as:

- an exploratory mathematical framework,
- a recursive admissibility environment,
- a semantic stabilization framework,
- and a governance-oriented abstraction system.

SIR is not currently presented as:

- a completed physical theory,
- a finalized cognitive model,
- a replacement for established mathematics,
- or a universal explanatory system.

---

# 4. Publication Philosophy

The repository intentionally separates:

- canonical structures,
- exploratory developments,
- governance layers,
- and application-oriented investigations.

This separation exists to preserve:

- mathematical consistency,
- semantic stability,
- publication clarity,
- and long-term structural integrity.

---

# 5. Canonical vs Exploratory

## Canonical Layer

The canonical public layer includes:

- recursive admissibility principles,
- structural integrity concepts,
- recursive stabilization structures,
- semantic consistency conditions,
- topology-aware recursive mappings,
- governance-oriented mathematical structures.

Canonical structures should remain:

- mathematically conservative,
- semantically stable,
- recursively bounded,
- and publication-compatible.

---

## Exploratory Layer

Exploratory sectors may include:

- advanced recursive mappings,
- speculative abstraction systems,
- semantic recursion experiments,
- operator-related investigations,
- higher-order recursive structures,
- and cross-domain exploratory models.

Exploratory sectors are not considered publication-stable unless mathematically consolidated.

---

# 6. Relation to TIG

Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG) remains the canonical physical admissibility framework.

SIR operates as a mathematically separated recursive abstraction environment.

The separation between TIG and SIR is intentional and preserves:

- scientific clarity,
- semantic consistency,
- publication stability,
- and controlled recursive development.

SIR should therefore not be interpreted as an uncontrolled extension of TIG.
