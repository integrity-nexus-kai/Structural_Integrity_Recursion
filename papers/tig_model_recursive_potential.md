# TIG Model Recursive Potential

## Abstract

This paper introduces a minimal recursive potential model associated with the spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The objective is to construct a mathematically analyzable effective recursive potential capable of generating bounded perturbative sectors within recursively constrained critical geometries.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No unique physical potential is currently claimed.
