# Yang–Mills SIR Research Program

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term exploratory research direction connecting:

- Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR),
- recursive spectral operator systems,
- variational lower-bound structures,
- and Yang–Mills-type spectral investigations.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is constructing a mathematically rigorous recursive spectral framework that may eventually become relevant for lower spectral investigations in non-abelian operator systems.

---

# Current Foundation

The present SIR framework already contains:

- recursive operator structures,
- recursive spectral analysis,
- variational lower-bound estimates,
- Hardy-type stability structures,
- recursive perturbative sectors,
- numerical lower spectral investigations,
- and recursive admissibility analysis.

These structures currently apply only to simplified recursive spectral operators.

---

# Core Research Direction

The long-term research direction investigates whether recursively admissible operator systems may naturally generate:

\[
\omega_{\min} > 0

through:

- recursive stabilization,
- admissibility-preserving evolution,
- variational lower bounds,
- recursive compactness,

# Separation Principle

A strict distinction must be maintained between:

## Recursive SIR Operators

Simplified recursive spectral systems investigated within SIR.

## True Yang–Mills Quantum Operators
