This paper investigates compactness structures and spectral boundedness properties associated with the recursive spectral operator framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on whether recursively admissible perturbative sectors generate bounded spectral domains under critical geometric evolution.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No complete spectral compactness proof is currently claimed.
