# TIG Gap Evidence Stability Tests

This paper investigates the numerical stability of the lower perturbative spectral sectors identified within the recursive spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on the robustness of the observed positive lower spectral behavior under:

- parameter variation,
- numerical resolution changes,
- boundary-condition modifications,

The objective is determining whether the observed lower spectral positivity represents a stable recursive structural feature or a numerical artifact of a restricted parameter sector.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No physical Yang–Mills mass-gap claim is currently made.
