# TIG Recursive Variational Spectral Framework

## Abstract

This paper presents the recursive variational spectral framework underlying Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The framework investigates whether recursively constrained critical geometries can generate stable lower-bounded perturbative spectra through non-linear geometric self-organization.

The objective is not to claim a complete quantum gravity theory or a solution to the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the framework establishes a mathematically analyzable recursive geometric structure connecting:

- recursive admissibility,
- perturbative stability,
- variational dynamics,
- topological consistency,
- and emergent spectral organization.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

---

# 1. Introduction

Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG) investigates recursively constrained geometric systems characterized by critical transition structures and admissibility-preserving evolution.

The central structural relation is:

\[
x^3 - x^2 + \beta^3 = 0
\]

with:

\[
x := \frac{r_H}{2M}
\]

and:

\[
\beta := \frac{r_c}{2M}
\]

The relation defines a recursive geometric transition structure exhibiting bifurcation behavior and critical stability sectors.

The primary scientific question concerns whether recursively constrained geometries can generate stable lower-bounded perturbative spectra.
