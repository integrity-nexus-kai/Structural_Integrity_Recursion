This document investigates whether multiplicative operator structure remains asymptotically stable under recursive perturbative refinement convergence.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is analyzing the central functional-analytic obstruction underlying asymptotic operator reconstruction:

\[
R_N(z)R_N(w)
\to
R_\infty(z)R_\infty(w).
\]

This document represents the first explicit product-stability phase of the refinement-stability program.
