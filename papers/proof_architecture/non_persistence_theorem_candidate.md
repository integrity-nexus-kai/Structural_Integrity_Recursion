# Non-Persistence Theorem Candidate

## Purpose

This document formulates the first explicit theorem-level non-persistence statement within the refinement-stability framework.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is expressing stabilization as the asymptotic non-survival of dangerous local analytical instability under recursive perturbative refinement.

This document represents the first explicit non-persistence-theorem phase of the refinement-stability program.

---

# 1. Motivation

Previous development progressively shifted the refinement-stability framework toward a more analytically realistic formulation:

- global perfect order appears unnecessary,
- catastrophic instability appears primarily local,
- recursive refinement may dynamically regularize dangerous spectral events,
- and stabilization may emerge through asymptotic non-persistence rather than strict suppression.

The framework now attempts its first explicit theorem-level formulation of this principle.

The central question becomes:

```text
Can dangerous local analytical instability
become asymptotically non-persistent
under recursive perturbative refinement?
```

The present document investigates this question directly.

---

# 2. Minimal Analytical Setup

Let:

\[
\mathcal{H}
\]

be a separable Hilbert space.

Let:

\[
T_N:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}
\]

be recursively refined self-adjoint operators satisfying:

\[
T_{N+1}
=
T_N+\Delta_N.
\]

Assume perturbation decay:

\[
\|\Delta_N\|
\le
\varepsilon_N,
\]

with:

\[
\varepsilon_N\to0.
\]

Assume weak local spectral regularity sufficient to exclude arbitrarily sharp persistent spacing-collapse spikes.

No uniform spectral gap is assumed.

# 4. Candidate Theorem

# Non-Persistence of Dangerous Local Instability

## Candidate Statement

Assume:

- recursive self-adjoint refinement,
- asymptotically decaying perturbations,
- weak local spectral regularity,
- and absence of coherent persistent instability concentration.

Then dangerous local instability cannot remain asymptotically persistent.

---

## Heuristic Consequence

Potentially:

\|(z-T_N)^{-1}\|

may exhibit transient local spikes,
while failing to remain persistently divergent asymptotically.
