# Non-Persistence Formulation

## Purpose

This document formulates the first explicit non-persistence framework for local analytical instability within the refinement-stability program.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is reformulating stabilization as the asymptotic non-survival of dangerous local spectral instabilities under recursive perturbative refinement.

This document represents the first explicit non-persistence phase of the refinement-stability program.

---

# 1. Motivation

Previous development progressively shifted the analytical interpretation of stabilization:

- global perfect order appears unrealistic,
- catastrophic instability appears primarily local,
- and recursive refinement may dynamically suppress persistent local pathologies.

The framework now reaches a critical conceptual transition:

```text
stabilization
may not require perfect asymptotic order,
but only:
non-persistence of dangerous instability.
```

This shift fundamentally changes the analytical objective.

The present document investigates this possibility directly.
