# Beyond Absolute Summability

## Purpose

This document investigates whether coherent amplification accumulation can remain asymptotically controlled beyond the regime of absolutely summable amplification sequences.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is determining whether robust asymptotic stabilization mechanisms survive under substantially weaker decay assumptions.

This document represents the first explicit beyond-absolute-summability phase of the refinement-stability program.
