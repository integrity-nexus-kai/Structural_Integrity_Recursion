# Almost-Everywhere Stabilization Theorem

## Purpose

This document formulates the first explicit almost-everywhere stabilization theorem candidate within the refinement-stability framework.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is expressing asymptotic stabilization as dominant regular projection behavior outside asymptotically negligible pathological refinement scales.

This document represents the first explicit almost-everywhere-theorem phase of the refinement-stability program.

---

# 1. Motivation

Previous development progressively transformed the stabilization framework toward a measure-theoretic asymptotic formulation:

- perfect global regularity appears unnecessary,
- dangerous instability may survive locally,
- pathological scales may remain asymptotically sparse,
- and regular refinement structure may dominate almost everywhere.

The framework now attempts its first theorem-level almost-everywhere stabilization statement.

The central question becomes:

```text
Can asymptotic projection stabilization
hold almost everywhere
despite sparse pathological instability?
```

The present document investigates this question directly.
