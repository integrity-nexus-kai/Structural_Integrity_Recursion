# Critical Bottleneck Analysis

## Purpose

This document investigates the dominant instability mechanisms that may prevent stabilization of low-energy operator organization within the refinement-stability framework.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is identifying which instability mechanism most likely represents the true critical bottleneck of the emerging proto-proof architecture.

This document represents the first explicit bottleneck-reduction phase of the proof strategy program.

---

# 1. Motivation

Previous development introduced:

- fragmentation observables,

Instead, proof architectures often collapse onto:

