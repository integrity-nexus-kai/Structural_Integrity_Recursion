# Almost-Everywhere Stabilization

## Purpose

This document investigates whether recursive perturbative refinement induces asymptotic stabilization on dominant refinement scales despite the continued existence of localized pathological instability.

The objective is NOT proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is reformulating stabilization as an almost-everywhere asymptotic dominance phenomenon in which pathological instability survives only on asymptotically negligible subsets.

This document represents the first explicit almost-everywhere-stabilization phase of the refinement-stability program.

---

# 1. Motivation

Previous development progressively transformed the analytical interpretation of stabilization:

- perfect global regularity appears unrealistic,
- instability may survive locally,
- pathological spectral events may persist transiently,
- and stabilization may emerge through asymptotic sparsity and non-persistence.

The framework now reaches a deeper analytical formulation:

```text
stabilization may dominate asymptotically
even when instability never disappears completely.
```

This suggests an almost-everywhere stabilization paradigm.

The present document investigates this possibility directly.
