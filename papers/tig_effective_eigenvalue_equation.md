# TIG Effective Eigenvalue Equation

This paper introduces the effective eigenvalue equation associated with the recursive spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The objective is to define a mathematically analyzable perturbative spectral equation governing recursively admissible fluctuation sectors within critical geometric backgrounds.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No complete physical spectral theory is currently claimed.
