# TIG Variational Gap Estimate

## Abstract

This paper investigates variational lower-bound estimates associated with the recursive spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on whether recursively admissible perturbative sectors satisfy positive quadratic-form bounds compatible with stable lower spectral organization.

Particular attention is given to:

- Hardy-type lower-bound estimates,
- inverse-square criticality,
- recursive stabilization behavior,
- and near-critical positivity structure.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No physical Yang–Mills mass-gap claim is currently made.

---

# 1. Introduction

The recursive spectral framework of TIG investigates whether recursively constrained geometric operators generate stable lower perturbative spectral sectors.

The effective recursive spectral operator is:

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{TIG}}
=
-\frac{d^2}{dx^2}
+
V_{\mathrm{rec}}(x,\beta)
\]

with recursive potential:

\[
V_{\mathrm{rec}}(x,\beta)
=
-\frac{\alpha}{x^2}
+
\lambda(\beta-\beta_c)^2
\]

The objective of the present work is establishing variational lower-bound estimates for recursively admissible perturbative sectors.

---

# 2. Quadratic Form Structure

The recursive spectral operator generates the quadratic form:

\[
Q[u]
=
\int
\left(
|u'(x)|^2
+
V_{\mathrm{rec}}(x,\beta)|u(x)|^2
\right)
dx
\]

Substituting the recursive potential yields:

\[
Q[u]
=
\int
\left(
|u'(x)|^2
-
\frac{\alpha}{x^2}|u(x)|^2
+
\lambda(\beta-\beta_c)^2|u(x)|^2
\right)
dx
\]

The positivity properties of this quadratic form determine recursive lower spectral stability.

---

# 3. Hardy Lower-Bound Estimate

The inverse-square contribution:

\[
-\frac{\alpha}{x^2}
\]

is associated with critical Hardy-type operator behavior.

For admissible perturbative sectors the Hardy inequality implies:

\[
\int |u'(x)|^2dx
\geq
\frac14
\int
\frac{|u(x)|^2}{x^2}dx
\]

Therefore:

\[
Q[u]
\geq
\left(
\frac14-\alpha
\right)
\int
\frac{|u(x)|^2}{x^2}dx
+
\lambda(\beta-\beta_c)^2
\int
|u(x)|^2dx
\]

This represents the primary recursive variational lower-bound estimate.

---

# 4. Effective Positivity Condition

The quadratic form remains positive if:

\[
\alpha < \frac14
\]

together with:

\[
\lambda(\beta-\beta_c)^2 >0
\]

outside the exact critical transition point.

Under these conditions the recursive operator admits candidate positive lower spectral sectors.

Potential positivity mechanisms include:

- recursive stabilization,
- perturbative localization,
- bounded spectral evolution,
- and admissibility-preserving operator structure.

---

# 5. Near-Critical Positivity Breakdown

Special attention is given to the recursive critical regime:

\[
\beta \rightarrow \beta_c
\]

Near this region the stabilizing contribution satisfies:

\[
\lambda(\beta-\beta_c)^2
\rightarrow 0
\]

The recursive spectral structure therefore approaches a critical positivity regime dominated by the inverse-square contribution.

Potential near-critical behaviors include:

- recursive spectral compression,
- perturbative destabilization,
- critical lower-bound weakening,
- and recursive localization enhancement.

The precise asymptotic positivity structure remains an open problem.
