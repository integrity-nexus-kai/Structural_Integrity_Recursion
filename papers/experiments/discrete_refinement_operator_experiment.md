# Discrete Refinement Operator Experiment

This document defines a first explicitly computable operator experiment for investigating refinement-induced fragmentation dynamics within the refinement-stability framework.

The objective is NOT modeling Yang–Mills theory or TIG directly.

Instead, the purpose is constructing a minimal discrete laboratory capable of testing whether recursive refinement generically induces:

- low-energy fragmentation,
- sector proliferation,
- spectral condensation,
- or instability of operator organization.

This document represents the first explicitly computable experimental layer of the refinement-stability program.

\(L_N\) represents a discrete Laplace-type structure.

For example:

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

-1 & 2 & -1 & \cdots \\
0 & -1 & 2 & \cdots \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots

This provides a controlled low-energy spectral baseline.

## B. Refinement Perturbation \(W_N\)

\(W_N\) represents refinement-induced structural perturbations.

Potentially:

\[
W_N
=
\sum_{k=1}^{M_N}
\epsilon_N U_k.
\]

Here:

- \(U_k\) are localized perturbation structures,
- \(M_N\) grows under refinement,
- and:

\[
\epsilon_N\rightarrow0.
\]

Thus:

- perturbations individually weaken,
- while structural complexity increases.
