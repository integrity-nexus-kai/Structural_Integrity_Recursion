# Structural Dependency Map

## Purpose

This document defines the structural dependency architecture of the exploratory Yang–Mills Interface framework within Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is maintaining:

- mathematical consistency,
- explicit dependency organization,
- controlled terminology,
- research transparency,
- and disciplined analytical development.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

---

# 1. Central Organizational Principle

The Yang–Mills Interface framework is organized as a layered exploratory research architecture.

The objective is not producing isolated speculative documents.

Instead, the framework attempts to construct a coherent continuum operator research structure involving:

- operator foundations,
- recursive stabilization mechanisms,
- perturbative control structures,
- continuum spectral organization,
- gauge-sector compatibility,
- and physically interpretable lower spectral behavior.

- recursively stabilized continuum operators numerically exhibit persistent lower spectral organization,
- recursively admissible stabilization structures appear analytically plausible,
- and recursively stabilized continuum evolution may remain compatible with bounded lower spectral behavior within simplified continuum operator systems.
