# Yang–Mills Self-Adjointness

## Abstract

This document investigates exploratory self-adjoint operator structures potentially relevant for future Yang–Mills spectral analysis within the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a rigorous construction of a Yang–Mills Hamiltonian.

Instead, the purpose is identifying the operator-domain, gauge-consistency, and spectral-stability requirements necessary before any mathematically meaningful lower spectral investigation can be attempted.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Introduction

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is fundamentally an operator-theoretic and spectral problem.

Any rigorous spectral analysis requires a mathematically admissible quantum Hamiltonian:

\[
H_{\mathrm{YM}}
\]

acting on a physically meaningful Hilbert space.

One of the central mathematical requirements is self-adjointness.

Without self-adjoint operator structure there is no rigorous guarantee of:

- real spectral values,
- stable quantum evolution,
- unitary dynamics,
- or mathematically controlled spectral behavior.

The present work investigates the foundational requirements for admissible Yang–Mills self-adjoint operator structure.

# 2. Yang–Mills Operator Structure

Yang–Mills theory is based on non-abelian gauge fields satisfying:

\[
D_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=0
\]

with field strength tensor:

\[
F_{\mu\nu}
=
\partial_\mu A_\nu
-
\partial_\nu A_\mu
+
[A_\mu,A_\nu]
\]

The non-linear commutator structure generates highly non-trivial operator interactions.

Any quantum Yang–Mills Hamiltonian must therefore incorporate:

- non-linear gauge interactions,
- gauge constraints,
- admissible physical state sectors,
- and renormalization-consistent operator dynamics.

The present framework does not yet construct such an operator rigorously.

# 4. Self-Adjointness Conditions

A physically meaningful Yang–Mills Hamiltonian must satisfy:

\[
\langle \psi,H_{\mathrm{YM}}\phi\rangle
=
\langle H_{\mathrm{YM}}\psi,\phi\rangle
\]

for admissible quantum states:

\[
\psi,\phi
\in
\mathcal{D}(H_{\mathrm{YM}})
\]

where:

\[
\mathcal{D}(H_{\mathrm{YM}})
\]

denotes the admissible operator domain.

The existence of such self-adjoint structures remains mathematically difficult for non-linear gauge systems.
