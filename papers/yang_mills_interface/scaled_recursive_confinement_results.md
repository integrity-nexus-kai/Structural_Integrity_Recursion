# Scaled Recursive Confinement Results

## Abstract

This document summarizes the first continuum-scaled recursive confinement investigations performed within the exploratory Yang–Mills Interface program of the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is studying whether recursively stabilized and continuum-scaled operator systems preserve:

- positive low spectral sectors,
- persistent spectral separation,
- bounded recursive spectral evolution,
- and stable gap-like spectral organization

under increasing operator dimension.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

The numerical investigation studied continuum-scaled recursively perturbed matrix operators of the form:

\[

+
V_{\mathrm{rec}}
+
V_{\mathrm{conf}}

\mu I

\[

2I - T_+ - T_-

dx^2

with:

\[
dx = \frac{1}{N+1}
\]

The recursive perturbation sector was:

\[
V_{\mathrm{rec}}(x,\epsilon)
=

+
\frac{0.25}{1+10x^2}

The recursive confinement structure was:

\[
V_{\mathrm{conf}}(x)
=
\kappa (x-0.5)^2
\]

The stabilization parameters were:

\[
\mu = 1.25
\]

and:

\[
\kappa = 12.0
\]

The operator dimensions investigated were:

\[
N = 20,40,80,160,320
\]

The exploratory numerical investigation indicates:

- persistent positivity of the lowest spectral sector,
