# Spectral Crowding Instability Analysis

## Purpose

This worksheet investigates the principal instability mechanism currently threatening spectral-gap persistence within the simplified continuum framework of the Yang–Mills Interface program.

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is isolating the phenomenon of asymptotic spectral crowding and analyzing why compactness persistence alone may still fail to preserve spectral separation.

This document identifies the central destabilization mechanism of the entire spectral program.
