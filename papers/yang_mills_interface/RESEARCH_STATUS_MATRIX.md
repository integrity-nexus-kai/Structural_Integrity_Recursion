# Research Status Matrix

## Purpose

This document defines the research-status classification framework of the exploratory Yang–Mills Interface program within Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is maintaining:

- explicit scientific transparency,
- disciplined claim separation,
- controlled analytical scope,
- auditability of research status,
- and avoidance of overclaiming.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

---

# 1. Status Classification Principle

Every major document and analytical structure within the Yang–Mills Interface framework should be interpreted according to explicit research-status categories.

The objective is distinguishing between:

- numerical observations,
- heuristic interpretation,
- analytical plausibility,
- exploratory mathematical structure,
- and rigorous theorem-level results.

The framework intentionally avoids unsupported claims.

# 8. Global Scientific Position

The scientifically correct global position of the Yang–Mills Interface framework is:

- recursively stabilized continuum operators numerically exhibit persistent lower spectral organization,
- recursively admissible stabilization structures appear analytically plausible within simplified operator systems,
- and exploratory lower spectral persistence mechanisms may remain compatible with bounded continuum-scaled evolution.

However, the framework currently does NOT establish:

- rigorous Yang–Mills Hamiltonians,
- rigorous gauge Hilbert spaces,
- rigorous renormalization-group flow,
- rigorous continuum-limit stability,
- rigorous confinement theorems,
- rigorous excitation-spectrum analysis,
- rigorous observable algebras,
- rigorous dynamical mass generation,
- or rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proofs.

---

# 9. Structural Transparency Principle

All future development within the Yang–Mills Interface framework should maintain explicit distinction between:

1. numerical evidence,
2. heuristic interpretation,
3. exploratory analytical structure,
4. partial mathematical plausibility,
5. and rigorous theorem-level results.

The framework intentionally avoids unsupported physical or mathematical claims.

---

# 10. Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective is constructing a mathematically disciplined continuum operator framework investigating whether recursively admissible stabilization structures may support:

- stable lower spectral persistence,
- bounded excitation organization,
- finite correlation-length behavior,
- gauge-consistent observable evolution,
- and dynamically generated effective-mass structures

within non-linear gauge systems.

At the current stage, the framework remains exploratory mathematical research only.
