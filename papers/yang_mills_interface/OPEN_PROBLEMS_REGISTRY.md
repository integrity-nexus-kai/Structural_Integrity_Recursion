# Open Problems Registry

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical registry of currently unresolved mathematical problems within the Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is explicitly identifying the principal unresolved analytical, spectral, continuum, and structural problems currently limiting the framework.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

---

# 1. Central Principle

A mathematically serious research program must explicitly identify:

- what has been rigorously established,
- what remains heuristic,
- and what problems remain fundamentally unresolved.

The present registry defines the primary open mathematical problems currently identified within the framework.
