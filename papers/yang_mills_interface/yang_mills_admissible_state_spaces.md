# Yang–Mills Admissible State Spaces

## Abstract

This document investigates exploratory admissible-state-space structures potentially relevant for future Yang–Mills spectral analysis within the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is studying how admissibility conditions, gauge constraints, recursive stability structures, and perturbative state selection may influence spectral organization in non-linear gauge systems.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Introduction

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is not only an operator problem but also a state-space problem.

Any rigorous spectral investigation requires identification of physically admissible quantum states.

The spectral properties of a quantum operator depend fundamentally on:

- admissible Hilbert-space structure,
- gauge constraints,
- physical-state selection,
- and perturbative sector organization.

The present work investigates whether recursive admissibility structures developed within SIR may eventually provide useful mathematical tools for studying admissible perturbative organization in non-linear gauge systems.

The interaction between gauge constraints and spectral structure is highly non-trivial.
