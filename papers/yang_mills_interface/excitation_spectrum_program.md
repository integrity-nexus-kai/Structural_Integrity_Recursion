# Excitation Spectrum Program

## Purpose

This document defines the exploratory excitation-spectrum program within the Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not claiming a rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is investigating whether recursively stabilized continuum operator systems may support:

- admissible excitation sectors,
- bounded excitation-energy organization,
- vacuum-relative spectral separation,
- and stable localized excitation structure

within gauge-consistent continuum operator frameworks.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

# 1. Central Problem

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is fundamentally connected to the structure of physically admissible excitation spectra above the vacuum sector.

The central spectral question is whether arbitrarily small admissible excitations may exist above the physical vacuum.

Equivalently, the framework investigates whether:

\[
\lambda_1-\lambda_0>0
\]

may remain stable under admissible continuum evolution.

The present framework therefore investigates excitation-relative lower spectral organization rather than absolute spectral positivity alone.
