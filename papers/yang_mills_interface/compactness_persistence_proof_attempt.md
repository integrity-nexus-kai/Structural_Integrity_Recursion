# Compactness Persistence Proof Attempt

## Purpose

This document presents an exploratory proof attempt for compactness persistence within the Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is investigating whether stabilization-oriented continuum operator structures may suppress spectral escape and preserve sufficiently localized low-energy spectral organization under continuum refinement.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No rigorous compactness-persistence theorem is currently claimed.

---

# 1. Central Problem

The principal continuum instability scenario identified within the framework is:

\[
\lambda_1(N)-\lambda_0(N)\rightarrow0
\]

as:

\[
N\rightarrow\infty
\]

even if every individual operator:

\[
H_N
\]

possesses positive spectral separation.

A major potential mechanism for such collapse is:

- delocalization of low-energy states,
- spectral diffusion,
- and escape of spectral mass toward increasingly diffuse continuum modes.

The present document investigates whether compactness and localization structures may suppress such destabilizing behavior.

---

The central exploratory principle is:

If:

- confinement localization remains sufficiently stable,
- perturbative boundedness remains controlled,

Assume confinement potentials satisfy:

\[

\[
