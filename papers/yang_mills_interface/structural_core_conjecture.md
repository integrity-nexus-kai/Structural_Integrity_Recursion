# Structural Core Conjecture

## Purpose

This document defines the central exploratory structural conjecture of the Yang–Mills Interface framework within Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is identifying a candidate structural principle potentially capable of supporting continuum-stable vacuum-relative spectral separation under admissible continuum refinement.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

The central continuum question is whether:

\[

The central exploratory hypothesis of the framework is:

Continuum-stable vacuum-relative spectral separation may require simultaneous persistence of several mutually reinforcing stabilization structures.

The framework suggests that no single mechanism alone is likely sufficient.

Instead, stable lower spectral organization may emerge only through combined structural control.
