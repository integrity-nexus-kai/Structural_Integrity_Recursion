# Yang–Mills Recursive Localization

## Abstract

This document investigates exploratory recursive localization structures potentially relevant for future Yang–Mills spectral analysis within the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is studying whether recursively admissible operator systems may generate stabilized localized perturbative sectors, bounded spectral organization, and admissibility-preserving recursive confinement-like structures relevant for non-linear gauge spectral analysis.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is fundamentally connected to the organization of perturbative excitation sectors and spectral stability structures.

One central spectral question concerns whether physically admissible perturbative sectors remain sufficiently localized to support stable lower spectral organization.

Localization structures frequently influence:

- spectral stability,
- discrete spectral formation,

- and bounded operator evolution.

The present work investigates whether recursive localization structures developed within SIR may eventually provide mathematically useful insight into non-linear gauge spectral organization.
