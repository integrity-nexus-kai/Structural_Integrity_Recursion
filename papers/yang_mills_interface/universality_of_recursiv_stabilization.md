# Universality of Recursive Stabilization

## Purpose

This document defines the exploratory universality program for recursively stabilized continuum operators within the Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not claiming a rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is investigating whether recursively stabilized lower spectral organization may remain robust under variations of:

- confinement structure,
- perturbative organization,
- boundary conditions,
- continuum scaling,
- and admissible operator evolution.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Central Problem

The current continuum-scaled recursive confinement investigations numerically exhibited:

- persistent lower spectral positivity,
- bounded recursive spectral evolution,
- stable lower spectral separation,
- and absence of spectral collapse under increasing operator dimension.

However, the current stabilization behavior was observed primarily within specific simplified recursive confinement models.

The central analytical question is therefore whether the observed stabilization behavior represents:

- model-specific numerical tuning,
