# Mathematical Identity Statement

## Purpose

This document defines the mathematical identity of the Yang–Mills Interface framework within Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is explicitly clarifying the mathematical nature, analytical scope, structural orientation, and scientific limitations of the framework.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

---

# 1. Central Motivation

The Yang–Mills Interface framework has progressively developed:

- continuum operator constructions,
- lower spectral investigations,
- perturbative boundedness analysis,
- self-adjointness structures,
- discrete spectral-gap analysis,
- continuum-gap persistence investigations,
- stabilization hypotheses,
- compactness and localization programs,
- and exploratory proof architectures.

As the framework expands, explicit clarification of its mathematical identity becomes necessary.

The objective of the present document is defining what the framework mathematically is — and what it is not.

# 2. Core Mathematical Identity

The Yang–Mills Interface framework is best understood as:

> an exploratory operator-theoretic continuum program investigating whether stabilization-oriented spectral structures may support continuum-stable vacuum-relative spectral separation under admissible continuum refinement.

The framework is fundamentally:

- operator-theoretic,
- spectral-analytic,
- stabilization-oriented,
- continuum-focused,
- and functional-analysis-based.

The present framework primarily operates within:
