# Master Research Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term research roadmap of the exploratory Yang–Mills Interface framework within Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is maintaining:

- explicit long-term research structure,
- disciplined analytical development,
- transparent scientific scope,
- controlled framework evolution,
- and mathematically consistent research organization.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.
