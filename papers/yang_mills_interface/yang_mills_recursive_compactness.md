# Yang–Mills Recursive Compactness

## Abstract

This document investigates exploratory recursive compactness structures potentially relevant for future Yang–Mills spectral analysis within the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is studying whether recursively admissible operator systems may generate compact perturbative sectors, bounded spectral organization, and stabilized recursive localization structures relevant for non-linear gauge spectral analysis.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Introduction

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is fundamentally connected to the organization of operator spectra and admissible perturbative state sectors.

One central mathematical question concerns whether physically admissible perturbative sectors remain sufficiently controlled to generate stable lower spectral organization.

Compactness structures often play a critical role in spectral stability, perturbative localization, and discrete spectral formation.

The present work investigates whether recursive compactness structures developed within SIR may eventually provide mathematically useful insight into non-linear gauge spectral organization.
