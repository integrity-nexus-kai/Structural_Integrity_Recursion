# Yang–Mills Recursive Spectral Stabilization

## Abstract

This document investigates exploratory recursive spectral stabilization structures potentially relevant for future Yang–Mills spectral analysis within the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework.

The objective is not claiming a Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof.

Instead, the purpose is studying whether recursively admissible operator systems may generate stabilized lower spectral sectors through recursive localization, compact perturbative organization, bounded spectral flow, and admissibility-preserving operator evolution.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Introduction

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem is fundamentally connected to the long-term stability and organization of non-linear operator spectra.

Stable lower spectral sectors require more than isolated eigenvalues.

They additionally require:

- stable operator domains,
- bounded perturbative evolution,
- admissible state-sector organization,
- recursive localization,
- and controlled spectral flow.

The present work investigates whether recursive spectral stabilization structures developed within SIR may eventually provide mathematically useful insight into non-linear gauge spectral organization.
