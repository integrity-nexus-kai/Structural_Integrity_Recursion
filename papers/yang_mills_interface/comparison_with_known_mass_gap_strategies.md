# Comparison With Known Mass-Gap Strategies

## Purpose

This document compares the exploratory Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) with major existing research directions related to the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

The objective is not claiming equivalence with established mathematical programs.

Instead, the purpose is identifying:

- conceptual overlaps,
- structural differences,
- complementary perspectives,
- and possible analytical relationships

between the present framework and known mass-gap research strategies.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

No rigorous Yang–Mills Mass Gap proof is currently claimed.

The Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem has historically been approached through multiple major mathematical and physical research programs.
