# Continuum Gap Stability Program

## Purpose

This document defines the exploratory continuum-gap-stability program within the Yang–Mills Interface framework of Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR).

The objective is not proving the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

Instead, the purpose is investigating whether rigorous spectral-gap structures established for simplified continuum model operators may remain stable under continuum refinement and scaling.

The framework remains exploratory and mathematically structural.

---

# 1. Central Problem

The previous spectral analysis established a rigorous positive spectral gap for a simplified continuum Schrödinger operator on a compact interval.

More precisely:

\[
\lambda_1-\lambda_0>0
\]

was established for a fixed operator system.

However, a fixed finite-dimensional or compact-domain gap does NOT automatically imply continuum-stable gap persistence.

The central analytical problem is therefore:

\[
\text{Does the spectral gap remain positive under continuum refinement?}
\]

# 3. Central Gap-Stability Question

The critical analytical question is whether:

\[
\lambda_1(N)-\lambda_0(N)
\]

remains bounded away from zero as:

\[
N\rightarrow\infty
\]

Potential outcomes include:

---

## Stable Gap Persistence

\[
\inf_N
\left(
\lambda_1(N)-\lambda_0(N)
\right)
>0
\]

corresponding to continuum-stable lower spectral separation.

---

## Gap Collapse

\[
\lambda_1(N)-\lambda_0(N)\rightarrow0
\]

as:

\[
N\rightarrow\infty
\]

corresponding to destabilization of vacuum-relative spectral separation.
