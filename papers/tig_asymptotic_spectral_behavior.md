# TIG Asymptotic Spectral Behavior

## Abstract

This paper investigates the asymptotic spectral behavior associated with the recursive spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on the asymptotic properties of recursively admissible perturbative sectors governed by the effective spectral operator:

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{TIG}}
=
-\Delta
+
V_{\mathrm{rec}}(x,\beta)
\]

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No complete asymptotic spectral proof is currently claimed.
