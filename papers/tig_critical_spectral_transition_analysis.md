# TIG Critical Spectral Transition Analysis

## Abstract

This paper investigates the critical spectral transition structure associated with Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on the recursive transition relation:

\[
x^3 - x^2 + \beta^3 = 0
\]

and examines whether near-critical recursive geometries generate structurally bounded perturbative spectral sectors.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No physical mass-gap claim is currently made.

# 2. Structural Polynomial Analysis

Define:

\[
P(x,\beta)
=
x^3 - x^2 + \beta^3
\]

Critical recursive transition behavior occurs when:

\[
P(x,\beta)=0
\]

and simultaneously:

\[
\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}=0
\]

The derivative is:

\[
\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}
=
3x^2 - 2x
\]

yielding critical points:

\[
x=0
\]

and:

\[
x_c=\frac{2}{3}
\]

The physically relevant transition sector is associated with:

\[
x_c=\frac{2}{3}
\]
