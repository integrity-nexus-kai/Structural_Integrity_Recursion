# TIG Lower Spectral Bound Estimates

## Abstract

This paper investigates lower spectral bound estimates associated with the recursive spectral framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on whether the TIG effective spectral operator admits positive lower spectral sectors under recursively admissible perturbative evolution.

Particular attention is given to inverse-square criticality, Hardy-type stability estimates, and near-critical recursive stabilization behavior.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No physical mass-gap claim is currently made.

# 4. Hardy-Type Stability Estimate

The inverse-square contribution:

\[
-\frac{\alpha}{x^2}
\]

The classical Hardy inequality implies:

\[
\int |u'(x)|^2dx
\geq
\frac14
\int
\frac{|u(x)|^2}{x^2}dx
\]

for admissible perturbative sectors.

Therefore the recursive spectral operator may remain lower bounded if:

\[
\alpha < \frac14
\]

This condition represents a candidate recursive stability threshold.
