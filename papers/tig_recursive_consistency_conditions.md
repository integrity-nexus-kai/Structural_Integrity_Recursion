# TIG Recursive Consistency Conditions

## Abstract

This paper investigates the recursive consistency conditions underlying the mathematical framework of Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The analysis focuses on whether the recursive spectral, variational, energetic, topological, and perturbative structures remain mutually compatible under admissible geometric evolution.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No complete mathematical consistency proof is currently claimed.

---
