## Status and Claim Mapping

The SIR labels `canonical`, `exploratory`, `speculative`, and `deprecated` describe local artifact curation. They do not replace Claim Status, Scientific Status, Question State, maturity, release state, or global synchronization. A curation label cannot promote a mathematical statement.

## Literature and Citation Inventory

SIR currently contains a Markdown paper and research-note collection but no declared publication-specific `.bib`. No empty bibliography is created merely to satisfy a filename rule. Before any paper is represented as release-ready, it must declare its own verified bibliography or a reproducible mapping to a repository bibliography, resolve every citation, record the literature-search scope and contrary evidence, and distinguish original SIR claims from established mathematics.

The root `CITATION.cff` is the sole repository citation identity. The lowercase `citation.cff` conflicts on license and must be removed.
