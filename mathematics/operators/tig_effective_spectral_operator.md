# TIG Effective Spectral Operator

## Purpose

This document introduces the effective spectral operator framework associated with recursive perturbative dynamics in Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG).

The objective is to define a mathematically analyzable operator structure governing admissible perturbative spectra within recursively constrained critical geometries.

The framework remains exploratory and structural.

No complete quantum gravity operator formalism is currently claimed.

---

# Conceptual Foundation

TIG assumes that recursive geometric integrity constraints influence the admissibility and stability of perturbative modes.

The recursive spectral framework is therefore represented through an effective geometric operator acting on admissible perturbative sectors.

The central objective is determining whether recursively constrained geometric structures can generate:

- bounded perturbative spectra,
- spectral localization,
- recursive stabilization sectors,
- or emergent lower spectral bounds.

\[

The parameter:

controls recursive geometric transition behavior and influences the effective perturbative structure.
