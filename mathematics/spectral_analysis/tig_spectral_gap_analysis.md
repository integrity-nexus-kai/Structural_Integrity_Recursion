# Mathematics Layer

## Purpose

The Mathematics Layer defines the formal mathematical foundation of the Structural Integrity Recursion (SIR) framework and its associated TIG structures.

This layer provides the rigorous analytical environment required to investigate:

- recursive geometric transitions,
- non-linear structural dynamics,
- operator stability,
- spectral formation,
- admissibility conditions,
- and emergent critical behavior.

The mathematical framework is designed to move TIG beyond heuristic modeling toward a formally analyzable critical geometric system.

---

# Core Mathematical Direction

TIG is treated as a non-linear critical geometric framework characterized by:

- recursive structural transitions,
- bifurcation behavior,
- topological admissibility constraints,
- and emergent spectral organization.

The central structural relation:

\[
x^3 - x^2 + \beta^3 = 0
\]

is interpreted as a critical transition equation governing structural phase behavior within the geometry.

# Scientific Objective

The long-term objective of the Mathematics Layer is to determine whether recursive geometric integrity structures can produce:

- stable critical transitions,
- emergent confinement-like behavior,
- and mathematically consistent spectral separation mechanisms.

This includes the investigation of possible relationships between TIG critical structures and broader non-linear field theoretic systems such as Yang–Mills-type confinement dynamics.
