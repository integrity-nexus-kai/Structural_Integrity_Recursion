# TIG Spectral Gap Analysis

## Purpose

This document investigates whether Topological Integrity Gravity (TIG) can generate emergent spectral gap structures through recursive critical geometric dynamics.

The objective is not to claim a solution to the Yang–Mills Mass Gap problem.

The objective is to determine whether TIG backgrounds exhibit mathematically consistent non-zero minimal fluctuation spectra arising from non-linear structural self-organization.

---

The central question is:

\[
\omega_{\min} > 0 \ ?
\]

where:

- \(\omega_{\min}\) denotes the minimal admissible fluctuation frequency,
- and the existence of a strictly positive lower spectral bound would indicate emergent gap-like behavior.
